# Devastation of Daggermark
Towards the end of the [[War of the beards]] the elven king [[Golarion]] led the nation of [[Lothern]] to cast the most powerful spell they could muster to finally destroy the dwarves. What happened next have many variations.

Some claim that it was a simple accident, too much power for the arch mages to handle. Others claim that one of the arch mages was a traitor who disrupted the ritual. Some claim the dwarves sabotaged the incantation in an unknown way. Some claim that [[Thirdenlin]] punished the elves for them trying to manipulate such magic.

Either way in the end the magic backfired and decimated [[Lothern]] and its people. Many of the elves including [[Golarion]] died. Most of the survivors were scarred and many fled and helped found [[Pitax]]. Of those whom remained many were further scared by the wild magic and presence of the [[Sleeping Realm]] turing into drow and kenkus, and they're hatred grew with the change.

The landscape that remained is what is now called [[Daggermark]] a nation rosen from those ashes. The land is now magically unstable, teetering on the edge of the [[Sleeping Realm]] and with a ragged starved landscape.
