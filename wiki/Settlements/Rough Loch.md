# Rough Loch
*The famed home of Locaths known for its [[Rare Materials|Shear Coral]] works*

Rough Loch is a village in [[Numeria]] that is entirely submerged in the ice cold waters of Beachshear. The village is a tribe lead by the most respected village elder.

Rough Loch is known its coral works made from the magical corals in Beachshear. And its special hardy foods. Much of the [[Rare Materials|Shear Coral]] is made into sculptures, special tools and weapons and statues.
## History 
Rough Loch has a proud history of independence and culture that many of its inhabitants take great pride in. They often therefore consider themselves subjects of [[Numeria]] only beacuse they want to.
## Economy 
Rough Loch uses the [[Rare Materials|Shear Coral]] to make a living with traders passing the nearby rivers. The village grows kelp and hunts fish to get the food needed.
