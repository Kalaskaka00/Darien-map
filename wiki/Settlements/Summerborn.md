# Summerborn
*Summerborn is the bustling city in the [[Golden Lands]]*, deep within the [[Stolen Lands]]

Summerborn is quite a small fortified city known for drawing unique and talented individuals.

Summerborn is known for its adventurers spirit and dangerous regions.
## History 
Summerborn was founded when the three adventurers [[Claire Nightshield]], [[Silvana Ashford]] and [[Bree Caley]] defeated the [[Stag Lord]] and took over his keep at the point called Stag Keep. They renamed the settlement and made it in to the home of the [[Golden Lands]].

## Important NPCs

- Councilors to the baronesses are [[Bille Frostbite]], [[Drift]], [[Kesten Garess]], [[Magrie Beeret]], [[Milo Grainseller]], [[Pixana Boldshard]], [[Zayne Copper]]
- [[Brave Wish]], [[Crash]], [[Siphon]] and [[Velmine]] are the most important military and guard force in town with [[Crash]] acting as Guard Captain.
- [[Bralon Alepike]], [[Felrick Kindhelm]] and [[Jelbi Flukeflow]] runt the most renown shops in town selling special items. The shops are called Runers Smithy and Flukeholm Tinkerers.
- [[Adsatra Sagebrook]] and [[Movh Bearer]] run the Sleeping Dragon Inn.
- [[Chirp]] and [[Errard Specher]] are quite loud people and have a tendency to mobilize public opinion.
- [[Brunno Meadowmane]], [[Garbin Sunheart]] and [[Laura Faelamin]] are more typical workers who make their living in Summerborn.
- [[n'Neb]], [[Reina Palett]] and [[Vacho]] are from other lands but currently make their home in Summerborn.

## Notable Locations 
- Stag Keep originates from the state of [[Oulry]] until it was taken over by the [[Stag Lord]] and later by [[Golden Lands]]. It is now serves as the government building ruling the barony.
- [[Claire Nightshield]]s grave is a small but strong tree commemorating the fall of one of the three first baronesses of the [[Golden Lands]]. It has become a site of pilgrimage for many druids and worshipers of [[Rendeen]].
- The Sleeping Dragon Inn is the most important Inn in town and tends to draw many visitors and residents alike. It is run by [[Adsatra Sagebrook]] and [[Movh Bearer]].
