# Varnhold
*Varnhold is a fortress town nested in to the mountains built from some old ruins.*

Varnhold is named after [[Maegar Varn]] and [[Vaegarian host]] whom founded the settlement

Varnhold is mostly known for the ruins and monsters surrounding it, not making it a settlement for those faint of heart.

## History 
Varnhold was founded in {{year:3862}} by the [[Vaegarian host]] with the blessing of [[Brevoy]] after they completed a great quest. The settlement grew in the rough lands and made an alliance with the [[Golden Lands]] for economic and protective reasons.

The settlement was wasted during [[Thundarak]]s uprising when the entire population was kidnapped. The people were only saved through the decisive actions of [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] with the aid of [[Derk]] and [[Peck Arck]].
## Economy 
Varnhold makes most of its wealth from the ore filled mountains and rare monster materials.

Varnhold has a harsh terrain not very suitable for agriculture, supplying food mostly though [[Summerborn]] and husbandry.
## Important NPCs 
- [[Maegar Varn]] is the leader of [[Varnhold]] and founder.
- [[Cephal Lorentus]] is the second in command and has been significant.
- [[Malora Grainseller]] and [[Ura Clawt]] are advisors in Varnhold.
- [[Daedove Wildguard]] and [[Peck Arck]] booth explore the area and have their base in Varnhold.
- [[Vondal Sandwashed]] is an artisan and builder in the settlement.
