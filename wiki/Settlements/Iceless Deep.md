# Iceless Deep
*The keep that descends the endless caverns and magma springs in the [[Boggy Ridge]].*

Iceless deep is a very deeply located settlement descending magma filled caverns. The settlement lives on its smithing and as one of few places that can produce [[Rare Materials|adamantine]] of high quality.

Iceless Deep is known for its smithies, the adamantine trade and their hot springs.

## Economy 
Iceless Deep makes most of its income in selling smithing weapons and rune magic. The food mostly come from trade and some mushroom grown in the caverns.
