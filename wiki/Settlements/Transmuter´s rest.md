# Transmuter´s rest
*The world leading city of alchemists and magical devices in [[Pitax]]*

Transmuter´s rest is an innovative city filled with alchemists, artificers and transmuters. It lays atop mines reaching rare arcane minerals.

Transmuter´s rest is among the best cities one can find if they are looking for potions, magical devices or arcane components. They are also known as being hostile to people not dealing with the arcane, treating them as second class.
