# Sandors Reach
*The Village at times called the home of [[Sandor]] at the edge of [[Mivon]]*

Sandors reach is a village built on the river and practically all its livelihood depends on it. The village lays on Sandors Walk and has some passing trade.

Sandors reach is mostly known for having some of the most earnest people in the realm.

## Economy 
Sandors Reach gain most its food from fishing in the river and growing crops along it. The village has a decent amount of trade that passes along the north of [[Mivon]]
