# Bleakwatch
*Bleakwatch is a minor settlement on one of the few agriculturally productive parts of [[Numeria]]*

Bleakwatch has some lands with the possibility to grow crops large parts of the year, even if it is only barley. It is quite a modest town between the [[Kings Road]] and [[Starfall]].

Bleakwatch is known for giving travelers the first impression of [[Numeria]], although it is usually not a good impression, thereby the name.

## Economy 
Bleakwatch relies on crop growth and some livestock for food and uses the nearby lumber and caves for resources to sell to merchants.
