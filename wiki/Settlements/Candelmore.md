# Candelmore
*Candelmore is the outpost of mages filled with research and innovation*

Candelmore is the mage outpost in a grand lake. 

Candelmore is known for their very skilled mages and their research.
## History 
Candelmore was founded in {{year:3541}} by a bunch of mages fleeing to do research in peace in the [[Stolen Lands]]. They enjoyed their outpost for a few decades before [[Nentrah]] and [[Xia]] cursed them into a time loop and isolation.

The outpost and inhabitants was however eventually restored by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] who integrated it into the [[Golden Lands]].
## Economy 
There is practically no economy in Candelmore and it relies on the economy of [[Summerborn]] to stay a float. The mages does however produce enough food to sustain those on the land.

- The [[Candle Mages]] of [[Annj Bearer]], [[Deren Seere]], [[Derk]], [[Flor Energa]], [[Ihara Absul]], [[Magie]], [[Whirl Palett]] and [[Yarslav Crain]] make their work in the place.
## Organisations/Groups
- The [[Candle Mages]] run the place and drive the innovation and research.
