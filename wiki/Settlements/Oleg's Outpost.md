# Oleg's Outpost
*The fortified village at the edge of [[Golden Lands]]*

Oleg's Outpost is a fortified farm stead which has steadily grown into a quiet village. The village lays at the edge of the [[Golden Lands]] and [[Brevoy]].

Oleg's Outpost is mostly known as a quiet village with goad hunting grounds for boar.

## History 
The outpost was founded by [[Oleg Crank]] and [[Gildi Crank]] in {{year:3837}} when they left [[Brevoy]] during [[The Rill War]].

The outpost would later become a part of the [[Golden Lands]] which led to its growth from a farmstead to a village.
## Economy 
Oleg's Outpost makes little money but works hard with farming, gathering and hunting and with the traders that pass by.
## Important NPCs 
- [[Oleg Crank]] and [[Gildi Crank]] founded the outpost.
- [[Sparrow]] moved to to Oleg's Outpost soon after it was founded and set up a workshop.
