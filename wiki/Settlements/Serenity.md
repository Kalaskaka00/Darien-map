# Serenity
*Serenity is the small fishing village surrounded by mists and cut of from the world on the edge of [[Embeth]]*

Serenity is a very isolated village that is surrounded by magical mists according to an old agreement with some hags. The village is also protected by the Behemoth that protects the river.

Serenity is virtually unknown to the outside world.

## Economy 
Serenity relies on fishing and some crops to feed their people.
## Important NPCs 
- [[Beheria]] and [[Oliveria]] are the hags that control the mists and protect the village.
- [[Odak One Eye]] is the chief of Serenity and [[Artarp Rein]] is his closest advisor and the mistspeaker.
- [[Yarek Gray]] is the bravest fisher of the village and the only person who dares to venture in to the mists.
- [[Ninne]] is an outsider of the village whom has been taken under its protection.
