# Iron Fork
*Iron Fork is a fortified city made to guard the border towards [[Daggermark]] and the [[Manchu Tribes]] and to protect the Barkway Creek*

Iron Fork is a fort city consisting of sailors, some daring traders and soldiers and those making a living of those things.

Iron Fork is known for its big gate spanning the Barkway Creek that can deny access and they use to charge tolls from the passing merchants. The city draws many odd characters due to its geography.

## Economy 
The city makes much of its money by charging passing ships and providing ship services. The city gets a good amount of food from fishing and supplements this with food from further down the river.
