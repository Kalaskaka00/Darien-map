# Nerrez
*Nerrez is the only city of [[Daggermark]] that is open for outsiders but it is strictly regulated and often described as dangerous.*

Nerrez is a market city and holds one of the greatest slave markets of the world. It sits on an island outside the mainland of [[Daggermark]] with a large natural harbor.

Nerrez is known as an exotic and dangerous place, most merchants whom pass through describe it as an odd and terrifying place, but the opportunities for profit can be immense.
## History 

## Economy 
Nerrez has practically no food production outside some fishing. The city relies on trade for its economy dealing mostly in slaves and selling services and rare items in exchange.
