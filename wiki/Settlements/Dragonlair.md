# Dragonlair
*Dragonlair is the city of war mages getting its name from many powerful elemental casters*

Dragonlair is a city steeped in tradition and hardened warrior mages and mercenary bands. It lays close to an old forest and the [[Boggy Ridge]].

Dragonlair is mostly well known for producing powerful war mages and procuring rare to find monster ingredients. They are known to look down their noses on non-battle magic. Many academies send their students to Dragonlair for some practical battle lessons.
## History 

## Economy 
Dragonlair gets their money from the rare monster hunts, mercenary work, teaching and from [[Pitax]] for serving as the main army. 

It does have its own food production that usually is enough to cover citizens demands relying on livestock and foraging mostly.
