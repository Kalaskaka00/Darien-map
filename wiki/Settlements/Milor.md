# Milor
*Milor is the capital of [[Mivon]] and is at times refereed to as the temple city.*

Milor is a city filled with paladins, clerics and priests to the point that people not involved with the church aren't allowed in to the center of town. It lays centrally in [[Mivon]] so that no one should be further from the Gods.

Milor is well known for its religious scholars, their pilgrims and for [[Milor temple]]. Many outsiders only know rumors of the place as the core of the city is closed of to those who are outside the church.

## Economy 
Milor stands mostly as a consequence of taxes paid by other cities and for their production of divine ingredients like holy water. The city has farmlands that produce enough wheat to feed the city with a small surpluss.

The closed of nature of the city means that traders and merchants are few.
