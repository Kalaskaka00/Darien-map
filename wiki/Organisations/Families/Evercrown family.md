# Evercrown family
*Stand proud Evercrown may the rest know of our accomplishements.*

Tve Evercrown family was for a long time an esteemed family in [[Pitax]] renown as the most skilled diplomats and skilled illusionists. They even held the place as the illusionist councilor as a reward for their efforts in negotiating the end of [[The Rill War]].

However the status of the family fell sharply in the {{year:3856}} as a scandal of dangerous experiments with the [[Sleeping Realm]] became public knowledge. The family managed due to their connections avoid a formal punishment but they lost their council seat to [[Farlamin Mistshaper]] and most of the family scattered to the wind. Now a days the family is but a shell of its former self and it can only operate behind the scenes due to its reputation.

The Evercrowns are generally known as a proud family with great skill with public speaking and negotiations.
