# Vein Grainseller
*Let´s keep it simple and quick.*

Vein is the head of the [[Grainseller family]] and mother of seven. She is very wealthy, she is however uninterested in politics and operations and only gets involved in the major decisions of the family. 

She is known to spend a good amount of time tending her own farm work and for her unbreakable will.

## Relations
- [[Milo Grainseller]] and [[Malora Grainseller]] are Veins children.
