# Yelgolor Remoudou
*Take a deep breath, enjoy the fresh air, feel the nature.*

Yelgolor is a wanderer, he has a home in the Stormy expanse of [[Numeria]] but spends as much time on the road as he does at home. He mostly lives of the land foraging and making a living for himself.

He is a man of great knowledge and wisdom, though one might not think of it so at the first encounter due to his fidgety nature and love of mushrooms. He is also known for spending great times crafting minor items whether they be boots or wood carving he spends great time and effort to get every detail right.
## Relations
- [[Claire Nightshield]] taught Yelgolor much about nature.
- [[Silvana Ashford]] was under Yelgolors care for some time.
- [[Shrowm]] and [[Brunno Meadowmane]] are fellow travelers whom Yelgolor enjoy spending time with.
