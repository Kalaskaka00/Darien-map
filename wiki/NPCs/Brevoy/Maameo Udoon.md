# Maameo Udoon
*If you keep annoying me I will crush your face.*

Maameo hails from the northern goliath tribes in [[Brevoy]]. She was a relatively unknown figure until [[The Rill War]] where she made her name as a fierce warrior.

Nowadays she is effectively the head of the [[Udoon family]].

## Relations
- [[Thorin Udoon]] is Maameos husband and the official head of the [[Udoon family]]
- [[Ekon Udoon]] and [[Kiri Udoon]] are Maameos children.
- [[Jamandi Aldori]] is a friend since [[The Rill War]]
