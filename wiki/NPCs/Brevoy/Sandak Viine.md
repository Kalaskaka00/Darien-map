# Sandak Viine
*My friend! How do you do today?*

Sandak is the head of the [[Viine family]] and is probably one of the most liked nobles in [[Brevoy]], especially by the people. 

He got the name Peacemaker after acting as negotiator of the peace deal after [[The Rill War]].

## Relations
- [[Keldithas Beeret]] is a rival of Sandaks.
- [[Calsys Viine]] and [[Emily Viine]] are Sandaks daughters.
- [[Rita Neat]] used to be in the [[Viine family]] employ until she went to work for the [[Talia Andureen|queen]].
