# Emily Viine
*I am sure we can strike a deal.*

Emily Viine is the youngest member of the [[Viine family]]. She is known as a skilled negotiator and for taking on many business trips as well as trade negotiations.

Emily is often described as quite a gloomy person with a love for haggling, trading and traveling.

## Relations
- [[Sandak Viine]] is Emilys father and the head of the [[Viine family]]
- [[Calsys Viine]] is Emilys younger sister.
- [[Claire Nightshield]] was a friend of Emily when she was younger.
