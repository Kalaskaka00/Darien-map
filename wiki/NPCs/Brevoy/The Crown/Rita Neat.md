# Rita Neat
*Another late night of work.*

Rita Neat is one of the [[Talia Andureen|queens]] highest rated administrators and at times serves as their proxy at different events. 

Rita is said to be very direct, efficient and formal. She has no noble titles and is rumored to have either ascertained her post through bribery, charm or raw talent.

## Relations
- Queen [[Talia Andureen]] is Ritas employer whom she serves loyally.
- [[Sandak Viine]] was Ritas previous employer when she served the [[Viine family]].
