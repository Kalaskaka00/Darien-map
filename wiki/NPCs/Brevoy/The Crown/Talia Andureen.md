# Talia Andureen
*Step forth and plead your case.*

Talia is a queen whom is known to care a lot for appearances and is always forced to balance politics between the noble families and regions to keep her kingdom together. She is known as a lover of her country and for finding good talent to surround her.

She has remain unmarried and has no legitimate heir leaving many rumors about her love life and whom may take her place next. 
## Relations
- [[Rita Neat]] is Talias advisor and one whom she respects highly.
