# Thorin Udoon
*How are you doing today?*

Thorin is known as a simple yet very likeable member of the [[Brevoy]] nobility. He often acts as the formal face of the [[Udoon family]], while his wife does a lot of the decisions.

He is the official Oathkeeper of [[Brevoy]] as he is liked by many and trusted by most.

## Relations
- [[Maameo Udoon]] is Thorins wife and the one who makes thing happen in the [[Udoon family]]
- [[Ekon Udoon]] and [[Kiri Udoon]] are Thorins children.
- [[Claire Nightshield]] was a friend to Thorin and a trusted advisor.
