# Reina Palett
*That's an inspiring landscape!*

Reina originates from Pitax where she became a powerful illusionist, but simply chose another life. Reina is an artist looking for inspiration and to sharpen her skill.

Reina nowadays dislikes [[Pitax]] as they always saw her skills as valueless.
## Relations
- [[Chirp]] and [[Magrie Beeret]] are creative friends of Reinas.
- [[Whirl Palett]] is Reinas great grandfather.
- [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]] used to be one of Reinas instructor in spell craft.
