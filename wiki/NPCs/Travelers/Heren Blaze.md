# Heren Blaze
*Do you want to do this the easy way or the charred way?*

Heren is an adventurer or mercenary who has made her name as a capable fighter and frightening foe. She is known for her temper sometimes earning her the nickname "Firebrand". She has fought in several conflicts and on most sides. She is not part of a consistent adventuring party.
## Relations
- [[Bille Frostbite]] is someone whom Heren has teamed up with quite a few times.
