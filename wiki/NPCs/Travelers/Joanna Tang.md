# Joanna Tang
*Why the long face, you make me think of a bok choy!*

Joanna is a master chef originating from [[Brevoy]] but who likes traveling on expeditions gathering food and such. Her cooking is thought to be some of the best in the country and plenty of noble families have tried to recruit her.

She is known as a happy person who can however be somewhat clumsy.

## Relations
- [[Claire Nightshield]] was a friend of Joannas, they booth aided in [[The Rill War]]
