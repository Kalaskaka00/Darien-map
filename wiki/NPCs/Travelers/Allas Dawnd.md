# Allas Dawnd
*Would you care to join me for a song.*

Allas is a traveling bard with a preference for the calm and serene nature. He occasionally claims to have nature as his audience. Allas originates from Tovette where he learned to play music and had a love for singning songs.

He travels with a small deer he calls Dambi.
