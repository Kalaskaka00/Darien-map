# Oliveria
*What was that? Do you hear them? The whispers.*

Oliveria is one of the two hags living by [[Serenity]] and maintaining the mists. Oliveria is said to be able to call upon souls to peak with them.

[[Beheria]] and Oliveria also have a "pet" monster called Scare.
## Relations
- [[Beheria]] is Oliverias sister and coven mate.
- [[Silvana Ashford]] is Oliverias estranged sister.
- Rabbarta was a sister whom was killed by the Jesters [[Harlequin]], [[Joker]] and [[Mime]].
