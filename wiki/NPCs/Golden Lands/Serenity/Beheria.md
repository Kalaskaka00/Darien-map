# Beheria
*Ohh dearies, what can I help you with?*

Beheria is one of the two hags living by [[Serenity]] and maintaining the mists. Beheria is said to chant powerful curses and spells.

Beheria and [[Oliveria]] also have a "pet" monster called Scare.
## Relations
- [[Oliveria]] is Beherias sister and coven mate.
- [[Silvana Ashford]] is Beherias estranged sister.
- Rabbarta was a sister whom was killed by the Jesters [[Harlequin]], [[Joker]] and [[Mime]].
