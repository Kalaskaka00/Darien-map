# Odak One Eye
*I may look old, but I can still swing a sword if the needs be!*

Odak is the chief of [[Serenity]]. He is known as a cunning and inspiring leader.

Odak has a raven with him called Mimic.
## Relations
- [[Artarp Rein]] is Odaks closest advisor.
- [[Yarek Gray]] is one of the old timers that Odak sometimes shares a drink and reminisces with.
