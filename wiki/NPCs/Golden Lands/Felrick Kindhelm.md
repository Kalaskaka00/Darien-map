# Felrick Kindhelm
*No invention is complete without a self-destruct button!*

Felrick is an inventor of renown who grew up in the [[Dwarven Keeps]] and made his name in [[Gralton]]. He lived in [[Iron Fork]] with his partner [[Jelbi Flukeflow]].

Felrick has a traveling background ,having seen much of the world. He is quick to point out that seeing problems and ideas is the basis of creating something new.

Felrick is known to be rentlessly curious hardworker staying up long into the mornings for some of his work. He is also known to be a perfectionist who is never finished more satisfied with his work.

Felrick eventually moved with [[Jelbi Flukeflow]] in to the [[Golden Lands]] on the request of [[Silvana Ashford]].

## Relations
- [[Jelbi Flukeflow]] is Felricks partner in work and life.
- [[Silvana Ashford]] is an old friend of Jelbi and Felrick.
- [[Bralon Alepike]] is a colleague whom Felrick likes to work with at times.
