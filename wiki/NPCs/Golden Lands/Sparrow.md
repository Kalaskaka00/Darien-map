# Sparrow
*Do you have any interesting ingredients to offer?*

Sparrow is an alchemist and herbalist whom made a new life for himself in [[Oleg's Outpost]]. His new life however quickly went wrong as he got addicted to hallucinogenics. 
## Relations
- [[Magpie]] inspired Sparrow to take on his new life and took him into the [[Doves]].
- [[Oleg Crank]] and [[Gildi Crank]] took Sparrow in and gave him a place in [[Oleg's Outpost]].
- [[Crash]] is a friend of Sparrows since his time protecting [[Oleg's Outpost]]
