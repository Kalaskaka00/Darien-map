# Wit
*Wanna make a bet?*

Wit is a lesser known tiefling from [[Dragonlair]]. He is known to be a bit of a handfull but nice charming guy.

Wit has a tendency to find himself in trouble with certain people while simultaneously being good at making new friends and acquaintances. He is also known to be the guy who can get into any party.

He often claims that he could make a living in any city, anywhere. In some of his more dunk moments he occasionally talks about how he will be someone who matters.

Wit eventually left [[Dragonlair]] and went to the [[Golden Lands]] to make a new life for himself where he amongst other things helped with a doppelganger plot.
## Relations
- [[Silvana Ashford]] is an old friend of Wit.
- [[Milo Grainseller]] is a friendly rival of Wits.
