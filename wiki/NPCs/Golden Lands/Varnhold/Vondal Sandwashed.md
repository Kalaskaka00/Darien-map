# Vondal Sandwashed
*Hmm, just because I am old doesn´t mean I haven nothing better to do than talking with yah.*

A stubborn dwarf mason who´ve spent much of his life building out of Sandstone in [[The Wastes]]. Vondal is a bit of a loner, he prefers his own company during a nice drink of ale in the night.

Respects hard work, dedication and perseverance. He has a ritual when he completes his work, where he lights a firepit in the building, and he adds something that makes the flames golden to bless the place.

Vondal would eventually move towards [[Varnhold]] where he would find much fine stonework.

## Relations
- [[Bree Caley]] was a pupil of Vondal as he taught much about his work and [[Ebus]]
