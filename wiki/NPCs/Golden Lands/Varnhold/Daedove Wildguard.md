# Daedove Wildguard
*There are plenty of incredible places to see, especially along the sea floor.*

Daedove is a member of the [[Vaegarian host]] whom originates from [[Embeth]]. She is half jokingly called the Vaegarian Amphibian specialist, booth beacuse her personal skills and her deep knowledge of oceans, weather and sailing.

Daedove is usually described as formal and direct.

## Relations
- [[Maegar Varn]] is Daedoves leader.
