# Malora Grainseller
*Sounds like it is a 1 in a 1000 chance to work, I´m in!*

Malora is the 6th child of the [[Grainseller family]]. She is in the family known for her adventorus spirit, she has been renown to travel far in search of buisness oppurtunities and adventures.

Malora is a bit of a black sheep in the family and spends little time involved in family affairs and rather focusing on her own enterprises.

Malora eventually traveled to [[Varnhold]] where [[Maegar Varn]] almost immediately made her into their treasurer.

## Relations
- [[Vein Grainseller]] is Maloras mother and the family head.
- [[Milo Grainseller]] is Maloras older brother and slight rival.
