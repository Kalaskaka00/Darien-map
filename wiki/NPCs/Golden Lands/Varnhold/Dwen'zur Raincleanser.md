# Dwen'zur Raincleanser
*I ask you don't reach for violence as a solution to your problems.*

Dwen'zur is one of the highest ranked shamans of the Centaur tribes he is known for his strict vow of no violence and avid follower of [[Ebus]]. He has a long running project of maintaining and improving the [[Bismuth Shrine]], the project goes from generation to generation.

Dwen'zur was greatly wounded in [[Thundarak]]s rebellion, but his strict non violence kept him from being turned himself. Eventually [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] aided him.
## Relations
- [[Egizaed]] and Dwen'zur occasionally exchange advice.
