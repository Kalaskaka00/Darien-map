# Ura Clawt
*That sounds like an intriguing challenge.*

Ura was a high ranking member of the [[Black Mantles]] conducting kidnappings in the [[Stolen Lands]] mostly direct towards [[Mivon]]. Her camp was eventually attacked and defeated by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]], [[Claire Nightshield]], [[Jonas Arc]] and [[Tiv]].

After going on the run Ura eventually became an advisor of [[Maegar Varn]] in [[Varnhold]].

## Relations
- [[Pixana Boldshard]] is a rival whom Ura likes to challenge and play mind games with.
- [[Maegar Varn]] is Uras boss.
