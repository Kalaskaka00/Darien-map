# Bralon Alepike
*This beauty will go for 200 gold, it is practically a steal if you look at its value.*

Bralon is a skilled smith who also dabbles in rune magic with a love for booth crafts. He is known to be quite social and friendly until one tries to sell something or buy something where he immediately becomes dead serious.

He used to run his own small smithy in [[Iceless Deep]] before he adventured to the [[Golden Lands]]. In the [[Golden Lands]] he set up a new smithy and sold to the baronesses [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]], [[Claire Nightshield]].
## Relations
- [[Laura Faelamin]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] are friends.
- [[Felrick Kindhelm]] is a professional colleague who Bralon enjoys working with.
- [[Annj Bearer]] has helped Bralon improve his understanding of rune magic.
- [[Claire Nightshield]] was a very close friend.
