# Peck Arck
*Oi! Look at this craftsmanship at least 300 years old, probably!*

Peck originates from the Dwarven Keep of Great gate where he was raised by a few gnomes. While raised he got his love for archeoligy and mechanical devices.

Peck is almost constantly traveling but became of more renown when he traveled to the [[Stolen Lands]]. While there he ventured into many ruins and sold some magical items to [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]].

Peck would eventually travel to [[Varnhold]] where he´d make a companionship with [[Conleth Umbra]]. He eventually encounter the dangers of [[Thundarak]], Peck would play a role in his defeat.

## Relations
- [[Conleth Umbra]] and Peck have aided one another in exploring some ruins in the [[Stolen Lands]]
