# Garbin Sunheart
*I'd be happy to he- Ouch, who put my rake th- Aww, since when is there a branch there?*

Garbin is a halfling farmer from [[Restov]], who had no farm or any equipment and had quite little experience. He is however a hard worker and optimist looking to find his place in the world.

Garbins family are successful farmers but he is seeking his own place in the world.

Garbin eventually ventured to the [[Golden Lands]] to make a place for himself.
## Relations
- [[Laura Faelamin]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] have gone through great efforts to teach Garbin how farming works.
- [[Calsys Viine]] saved Garbins life once when some bandits attacked.
