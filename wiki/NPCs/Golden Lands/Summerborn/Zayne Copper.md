# Zayne Copper
*Are you okay? It´s okay to stop and take a breath.*

Zayne is a retired mercenary. He is known for his big heart and for befriending anyone he meets. He has a wife and daughter.

After his life as mercenary he became an advisor in [[Varnhold]] to later become an advisor to the [[Golden Lands]].
## Relations
- [[Conleth Umbra]] is a friend of Zayne as they both adventured in the [[Stolen Lands]]
- [[Karri]] is a highly valued colleague.
- [[Maegar Varn]] used to be Zaynes boss when we was a councilor in [[Varnhold]].
