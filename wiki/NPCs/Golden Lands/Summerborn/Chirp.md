# Chirp
*Let the people sing and dance!*

Chirp is a woman with passions of many things, most things being silly, political or infuriating. She is the younger [[Pixana Boldshard|Boldshard sister]]. She is quite quiet about her origins but simply says she chose another path for life then the one layed out.
## Relations
- [[Pixana Boldshard]] is Chirps older sister.
- [[Magrie Beeret]] and [[Reina Palett]] are close creative friends.
- [[Errard Specher]] is a fellow speaker who brings truth to power.
