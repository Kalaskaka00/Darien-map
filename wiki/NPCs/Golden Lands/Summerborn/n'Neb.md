# n'Neb
*The only good troll is a dead troll.*

n'Neb is the only remaining member of the Spotted Tribe. He was until the end of their tribe the commander of the honor guard, the guard prescribed to protect the elders and village. n'Nebs tribe was killed by trolls, the gung lacked tools to kill the trolls. n'Neb was the only survivor of the raid and only because his enhanced weapon is acidic.

n'Neb went on a quest to track down and strike down [[Chorrad Troll King]] and [[Tandeen Dazzlehand|Marionette]] to get his revenge. [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] aided on this quest and later took n'Neb in.
## Relations
- [[Vacho]] is a friend from their time in [[Summerborn]].
