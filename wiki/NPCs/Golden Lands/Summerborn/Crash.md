# Crash
*How about a quick fight, arm wrestling match, boxing match? *

Crasus originates from [[Gralton]] where he first worked as a guard, he eventually got laid of for insulting his captain. After that he failed to find other employment and took up work as a ruffian.

Crash was one of the [[Stag Lord]]s lieutenants he is known to be quite a rowdy fella who can be as dangerous to comrades as he is to enemies.

[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] defeated him early on and convinced him to switch to side making him one of their earliest allies and a protector.

After aiding in defeating the [[Stag Lord]] Crash became the guard captain of [[Summerborn]].

Crash was severely wounded when a farm field came to life.

## Relations
- [[Siphon]] was someone Crash met on the road in [[Pitax]], he took her under his wing and has since been her protector.
- [[Kesten Garess]] is Crashes boss and someone who Crash has aided loyally.
- [[Bree Caley]] is Crashes preferred sparring partner.
- [[Oleg Crank]], [[Gildi Crank]] and [[Sparrow]] are friends since Crashes time protecting [[Oleg's Outpost]].
- Crash used to work for the [[Stag Lord]] together with [[Ox]], [[Bille Frostbite]] and [[Xer]].
