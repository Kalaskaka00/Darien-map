# Velmine
*Easiest way to get stronger is to fight.*

Velmine has a background as a mercenary operating in the Wastes. There are rumors that she has slayed several demons, but these seemingly have little basis.

Velmine was eventually contracted by [[Jamandi Aldori]] to travel to the [[Stolen Lands]], which they did with [[Brave Wish]] and [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]]. [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]] however hurt their minds until Velmine was freed by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]]. In return Brave helped to defeat the [[Stag Lord]].

When fighting a group of Trolls under [[Kesten Garess]] command booth her horns were broken off and she was severely wounded.

After the funding of the [[Golden Lands]] Brave would aid in various tasks and eventually go to [[Varnhold]] where he was trained by [[Maegar Varn]] to sharpen his skills.
## Relations
- [[Brave Wish]] is a warrior whom Velmine fought side by side with many times.
- [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] have aided Velmine greatly.
- [[Maegar Varn]] has been an important mentor.
