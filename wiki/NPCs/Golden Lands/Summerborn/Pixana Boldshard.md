# Pixana Boldshard
*With some adequate maneuvering we should be able to both put them were we want them and have them owe us a favor.*

Pixana is the oldest [[Chirp|Boldshard sister]] known for her calm demeanor and masterful people skills. Pixana speaks little about her and her sisters origins other than that they chose to respectfully leave.

She traveled with [[Chirp]] and spent time to keep her safe and making connections. She has through her travels made connections with many people and has used her memory and people skills to earn favors.

After making her way to the [[Golden Lands]] with her sister Pixana was promoted on to the council as a grand diplomat. A role in which she proved her self as very competent.
## Relations
- [[Chirp]] is Pixanas younger sister.
- [[Ura Clawt]] is a rival to Chirp whom she enjoys challenging to mind games.
