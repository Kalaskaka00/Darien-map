# Brave Wish
*One must keep ones instincts and mind as sharp as ones blade.*

Brave is a traveling warrior from the east. They're known as a promising swordsman with great skill and isntinct.

Brave was eventually contracted by [[Jamandi Aldori]] to travel to the [[Stolen Lands]], which they did with [[Velmine]] and [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]]. [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]] however hurt their minds until Brave was freed by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]]. In return Brave helped to defeat the [[Stag Lord]].

After the funding of the [[Golden Lands]] Brave would aid in various tasks and eventually go to [[Varnhold]] where he was trained by [[Maegar Varn]] to sharpen his skills.
## Relations
- [[Velmine]] is a trusted comrade in arms of Brave.
- [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] are owed a great debt of gratitude for all the aid they have given Brave.
- [[Maegar Varn]] has been an important mentor.
