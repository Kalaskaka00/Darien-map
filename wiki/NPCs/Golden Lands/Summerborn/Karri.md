# Karri
*If one wishes to tell others what to do one must first listen.*

Karri is a previous leader of the small Airat tribe from the [[Manchu Tribes|Manchu region]].  She eventually wandered east in search of new lands with less painful memories.

Karri eventually ended up in the [[Golden Lands]] were she became an advisor to the baronesses.
## Relations
- [[Zayne Copper]] is a highly valued colleague.
