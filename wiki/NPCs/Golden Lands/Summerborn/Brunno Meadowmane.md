# Brunno Meadowmane
*There's some good foraging this time of year.*

Brunno is a seasoned forester who spends most his time chopping trees, foraging and woodcarving. He traveled to the [[Golden Lands]] to see more of nature and live a calmer life.

In the [[Golden Lands]] Brunno aided the baronesses several times by acting as a guide and scout even getting in a fight with a troll at one point.
## Relations'

- [[Movh Bearer]] is a friend who Brunno enjoys sharing stories with.
- [[Shrowm]] and [[Yelgolor Remoudou]] are nice souls traveling the [[Stolen Lands]] whom Brunno encounters every now and then.
