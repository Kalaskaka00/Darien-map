# Drift
*I see plenty, but not through my eyes.*

Drift is an a priestess who has dedicated herself to [[Xiander]] and is said to have the power to see prophecies and futures. She originates from [[The Wastes]] where she has made a living as a guide and ceremonial master.
She eventually ventured to the [[Golden Lands]] where she became an advisor and gave several important prophecies.
