# Jelbi Flukeflow
*Have you read this book?! I am sure you would love it!*

Jelbi is a researcher from [[Gralton]]. She lived in [[Iron Fork]] with her partner [[Felrick Kindhelm]].

Jelbi has a traveling background ,having seen much of the world. She is quick to point out that by seeing more things one can have more ideas.

Jelbi is known as a woman with a seemingly infinite amount of simultaneous tasks which she quickly moves between, often at a pace where few others can keep up. She is known to be constantly inquisitive but simultaneously relaxed. She is known as an avid reader.

Jelbi eventually moved with [[Felrick Kindhelm]] in to the [[Golden Lands]] on the request of [[Silvana Ashford]].

## Relations
- [[Felrick Kindhelm]] is Jelbis partner in life and work.
- [[Iandri Flukeflow]] is Jelbis father and has inspired her in her pursuit of knowledge.
- [[Silvana Ashford]] is an old friend of Jelbi and Felrick.
- [[Annj Bearer]] is someone whom Jelbi likes discussing books with.
