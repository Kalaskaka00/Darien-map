# Errard Specher
*Centralised rule is simply a way to gurantee unjust and inefficent rule.*

Errard is a political philosopher son of the famous Claus Speecher from [[Gralton]]. He has become infamous for picking up his fathers work in abdicating for more democratic, equal and decentralized rule.

Errard has through his work been kicked out of most nations at different times and has only pennies to his name.
## Relations
- [[Chirp]] is a fellow speaker who tells the people what she thinks.
