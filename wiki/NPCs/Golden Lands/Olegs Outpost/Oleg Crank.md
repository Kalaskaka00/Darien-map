# Oleg Crank
*Hmmm, I swear the corn tasted better back in my day.*

Oleg is a settler, merchant and innkeeper at the edge of the [[Golden Lands]]. He and his wife [[Gildi Crank]] built [[Oleg's Outpost]] where they lived for a long time.

After Olegs and [[Gildi Crank]]s son died in [[The Rill War]] they chose to find a new life and go on.

## Relations
- [[Gildi Crank]] is Olegs wife.
- [[Sparrow]] and [[Crash]] are friends whom have helped the outpost.
