# Gildi Crank
*Weeds can be very pretty too, and edible.*

Gildi is a settler, forager and innkeeper at the edge of the [[Golden Lands]]. She and her husband [[Oleg Crank]] built [[Oleg's Outpost]] where they lived for a long time.

After Gildis and [[Oleg Crank]]s son died in [[The Rill War]] they chose to find a new life and go on.

## Relations
- [[Oleg Crank]] is Gildis husband.
- [[Sparrow]] and [[Crash]] are friends whom have helped the outpost.
