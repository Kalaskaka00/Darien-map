# Deren Seere
*When reading the weave I can see some disturbances.*

Deren is the master of knowing in the [[Candle Mages]]. Deren is the informal leader of the [[Candle Mages]]. He is renown for his serious and annoyed demeanor.

Deren and the other [[Candle Mages]] were cursed by [[Lord of Stories]] and [[Nentrah]] and fell into forgetfulness and underneath. Until [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] broke the curse (mostly) and got the [[Candle Mages]] active but stuck on [[Candelmore]].
## Relations
- [[Derk]], [[Flor Energa]], [[Ihara Absul]], [[Magie]], [[Whirl Palett]] and [[Yarslav Crain]] are fellow [[Candle Mages]].
- [[Annj Bearer]] is an apprentice to the [[Candle Mages]].
- [[Joker|Jandeen Faelmin]] used to be a member and friend in the [[Candle Mages]].
