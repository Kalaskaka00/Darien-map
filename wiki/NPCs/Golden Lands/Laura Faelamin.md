# Laura Faelamin
*The bees wont sting you, I promise, just don't make any sudden movements.*

Laura for a long time worked as part botanist and part beekeeper in [[Bleakwatch]]. She made her living on selling flowers, lending her bees to farmers and sometimes working as a veterinarian. She is known to be a happy and energetic person but that can easily get bored.

She would eventually move to the [[Golden Lands]] to aid [[Claire Nightshield]] in the funding of [[Summerborn]].
## Relations
- [[Bralon Alepike]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] are friends.
- [[Garbin Sunheart]] is someone Laura has spent great effort trying to tutor.
- [[Claire Nightshield]] was a very close friend.
