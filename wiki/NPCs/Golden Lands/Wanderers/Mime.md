# Mime
*Makes a small bow*

Mime is the oldest member of the Jesters and originates from the crusader state in the [[Stolen Lands]] from long ago. There she was one high ranking paladin and one of few survivors.

Mime kept watch during an assault in the [[Aldori family]] household where mime got into a fight with [[Conleth Umbra]] leaving Conleth seriously wounded.
## Relations
- [[Lord of Stories]] is Mimes lord.
- [[Harlequin]] and [[Joker]] are fellow Jesters.
