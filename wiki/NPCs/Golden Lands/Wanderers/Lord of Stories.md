# Lord of Stories
*You better have a good story to tell if you want my attention*

The Lord of Stories is the avatar of [[Xia]] a fragment of [[Xiander]]. Where [[Xiander]] at [[The Magical Anorchrism]] chose to split of his emotions to become an impartial judge [[Xia]] is what became of that fragment of [[Xiander]]. Where [[Xiander]] acts impartially in destinies [[Xia]] is more than happy to tip the scales to create a greater story.

The stories [[Xia]] creates in turn helps him gain power when they reach their end. To get more stories and end them he contracted [[Nentrah]] and got his Jesters as minions. When doing this work in the [[Stolen Lands]] [[Xia]] would also through a dealing steal much of [[Wyndee]]s power adding curses to his domain.

The Lord os Stories killed [[Claire Nightshield]] when she fell into his domain chasing [[Nentrah]].
## Relations
- [[Harlequin]], [[Joker]] and [[Mime]] are the Jesters whom act on the Lords wishes.
- [[Nentrah]] made a deal and has since then served the Lord of Stories.
