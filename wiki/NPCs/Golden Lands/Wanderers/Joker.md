# Joker
*Ladies and gentlemen, this may be our final act.*

Joker was the last joker to join the Jesters after leaving the [[Candle Mages]]. He was one of three "survivors" and in investigating the phenomenon he got in contact with [[Lord of Stories]].
## Relations
- [[Lord of Stories]] is Jokers lord.
- [[Harlequin]] and [[Mime]] are fellow Jesters.
- [[Deren Seere]], [[Derk]], [[Flor Energa]], [[Ihara Absul]], [[Magie]], [[Whirl Palett]] and [[Yarslav Crain]] used to be fellow [[Candle Mages]].
