# Nentrah
*You [[Waking World|True Worlders]] only destroy.*

Nentrah is a dryad from the [[Stolen Lands]] were she and her sisters lived until their settlement was destroyed by mortals from the [[Waking World]]. After almost everyone had been killed or captured Nentrah made a deal with [[Lord of Stories]].

Nentrah would spend the next 1000 years destroying kingdoms which would rise in the [[Stolen Lands]] including:
- A crusader state
- A cyclops kingdom led by [[Thundarak]]
- A [[Pitax]] colony
- The [[Candle Mages]]
- City state of [[Oulry]]
- The [[Stag Lord]] and his gang

Eventually when trying to fell the [[Golden Lands]] she was captured by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] during the siege of [[Summerborn]].
## Relations
- [[Lord of Stories]] is Nentrahs patron and their aides sometimes interact with Nentrah [[Harlequin]], [[Joker]] and [[Mime]]
- [[Myron]] is the only of Nentrahs siter who survived the fall of their home.
