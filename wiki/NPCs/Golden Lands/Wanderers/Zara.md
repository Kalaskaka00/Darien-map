# Zara
*Nothing beats siting outside with fresh game grilling in front of you.*

Zara originally hails from [[Pitax]] until she decided to venture to the [[Stolen Lands]]. While in the [[Stolen Lands]] she was inflicted with a lycanthropy curse turning her into a wewlynx with limited control and hazy memories.

[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] would however partially cure Zara granting her better control and slightly sharper memories. Zara however chose to keep the lycanthropy as it had become a part of her identity. Zara in turn helped the baronesses a few times with warnings and some aid in combat.
## Relations
- [[Siphon]] is a fellow free spirit.
