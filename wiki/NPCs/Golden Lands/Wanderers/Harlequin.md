# Harlequin
*Wanna play a game? I like games. How about whack a baroness?*

Harlequin was the second member of the Jesters. She originates from [[Pitax]] when they controlled  parts of the [[Stolen Lands]]. She was at the time a high ranking noble who on the verge of starving preyed to an almost unknown God of [[Xia]]. She was later brought to be part of his cause.
## Relations
- [[Lord of Stories]] is Harlequins lord.
- [[Joker]] and [[Mime]] are fellow Jesters.
