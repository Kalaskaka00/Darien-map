# Shrowm
*Hee? Want some mushrooms? They are slightly poisonous, but you'll probably survive.*

Shrowm is a very peculiar wanderer seemingly obsessed with mushrooms.
## Relations
- [[Yelgolor Remoudou]] and [[Brunno Meadowmane]] are fellow wanderers in the [[Stolen Lands]]
- [[Vacho]] is a lizardfolk whom Shrowm saved the life of once when he was poisoned.
