# Myron
*Silly true worlder, all your kind does is destruction.*

Myron is a dryad whom hails from the [[Stolen Lands]] and saw the dryads home fall. Myron doubts most [[Waking World|true worlders]] after having had several encounters where she was exploited.

## Relations
- Oak is a nature spirit bound to Myron.
- [[Nentrah]] is Myrons last living sister from the before times.
- The [[Stag Lord]] used Nentrah for her power and sealed her away in an amulett.
