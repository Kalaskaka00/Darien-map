# Aelthot
*Justice reigns supreme.*

Aelthot is one of the highest rated tribefathers amongst the Lizardfolk tribes. He leads the [[Gauch Tribe]].

Aelthot is known as a knowledgeable and just leader.
## Relations
- [[Egizaed]] has been a valuable advisor to Aelthot.
- [[Vacho]] is part of the [[Gauch Tribe]] and answers to Aelthot.
