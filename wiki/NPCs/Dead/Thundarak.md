# Thundarak
*I as Xianders chosen demand that you kneel before your betters!*

Thundarak was once the God King of the cyclops kingdom in the [[Stolen Lands]], with the [[Divine Artifacts|eye of Xiander]] his power was immense. Having transplanted the eye in to himself Thundarak belived himself to be a physical manifestation of [[Xiander]]. After being placed in a deep slumber Thundarak and a lot of his people withered away.

Thundarak would eventually rise again and take control of a large part of the [[Stolen Lands]] including [[Varnhold]].

Eventually [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] defeated Thundarak with the aid of [[Derk]], the [[Candle Mages]] and [[Peck Arck]].
