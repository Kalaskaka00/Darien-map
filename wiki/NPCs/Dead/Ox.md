# Ox
*To battle weaklings!*

Ox was the [[Stag Lord]]s top lieutenant  and is renowned as a fearsome warrior and capable tactician. He was known to be cruel and ruthless in his tactics.

Ox was eventually defeated by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] who convinced him to help take down and defeat the [[Stag Lord]], Ox however died during the fighting.
## Relations
- [[Stag Lord]] was Oxes former commander.
- [[Bille Frostbite]], [[Xer]] and [[Crash]] were fellow commanders to Ox.
