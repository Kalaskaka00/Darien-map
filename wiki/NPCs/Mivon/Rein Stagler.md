# Rein Stagler
*Let us all take a deep breath, speak as friend, not foe.*

Rein is the highest ranking cleric of [[Rendeen]] in Mivon. He is known to be a calm man.

Rein lost his legs during his childhood, although he usually prefers not to share any details about the event.
