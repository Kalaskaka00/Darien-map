# Barnur Keenhold
*I Shall eliminate the evil from this realm!*

Barnur is a highly respected paladin whom have dedicated themselves to destroying evil. Barnur is a dedicated follower of [[Dunden]] known for his direct and decisive demeanor.

Barnur made a name for himself as "The Great Sledge" during [[The Rill War]].

## Relations
- Barnur helped to train [[Jonas Arc]].
- Barnur had a friendly rivalry with [[Korag]].
