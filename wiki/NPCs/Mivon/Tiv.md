# Tiv
*We won´t falter!*

Tiv is a medium ranked paladin of [[Mivon]]. Tiv got her nickname after spending a month wandering through tundras for a quest.

Tiv was at a point cursed in the [[Stolen Lands]] until she was saved by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]].

## Relations
- Tiv has at times had a minor mentorship role towards [[Jonas Arc]].
