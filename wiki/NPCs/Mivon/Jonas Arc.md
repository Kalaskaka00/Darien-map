# Jonas Arc
*I shall protect my home from the wicked!*

At a young age Jonas was chosen by the Gods themselves to become a hero of [[Mivon]]. He has since that day been training tirelessly to live up to his destiny. He is known as a kind, brave and honorable.

Jonas wields [[Divine Artifacts|Dundens Divine Blade]] making him a terrifying force to face.

## Relations
- [[Silvana Ashford]] is a childhood friend of Jonas, they have aided each other a few times over the years despite their very different destinies.
- [[Tiv]] and [[Barnur Keenhold]] have booth mentored Jonas at different points.
