# Jarlek Grin
*Greetings friends! What do you say we take a drink and talk business*

An ever smiling cooperator Jarlek works in his word to getting the right people in the right room together and getting the right favors. He has thereby become a powerful force in [[Garent]] especially amongst merchants, nobles, artisans and adventurers.
## Relations
- [[Asha Rakker]] is a rival of Jarleks.
- [[Turtahir]], [[Meel'a]] and [[Agamoos Evercrown]] are business associates of Jarlek and they have helped each other out of a few scrapes.
