# Lalne Quill
*I am sorry can you repeat that? I wouldn't want to get anything wrong.*

Lalne is the Head Archivist for the council in [[Pitax]] meaning she takes notes and writes down decisions. In Pitax this is considered a prestigious role. 

Lalne is known as a quiet girl whom seems ever so slightly obsessed with noting down anything of value. 
## Relations
- [[Farlamin Mistshaper]] is one in the council whom Lalne serves under.
