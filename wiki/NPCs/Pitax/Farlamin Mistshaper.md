# Farlamin Mistshaper
*Greetings, an auspicious day to make your acquaintance, may this encounter mean good fortunes.*

Farlamin is one of the council in [[Pitax]] representing the Illusionist School in the leading chambers. In this roll Farlamin also leads the Illusionist School deciding research grants and matters of education.

Farlamin is a known as a clever strategist and cunning man who can use illusions magics to devastating efficiency. 

Farlamin fought in [[The Rill War]] known for altering enemies perceptions of key pieces of the battlefield.
## Relations
- [[Lalne Quill]] is the head archivist of the Council and a friend of Farlamins.
- [[Adsatra Sagebrook]] was Farlamins partner for a long time until she left [[Pitax]].
- [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]] was a student of Farlamin.
