# Kronharth
*Kronharth or formally the Divine Imperum of Kronharth is an empire made up of about 50 principalities and kingdoms all with their own rulers and interests. This combined with the weak influence of the Imperial Emperor means the nations is in constant internal struggles.

Kronharth is known as the home of chivalry, knighthood and adventures. This however often comes into conflict with the constant struggles and conflicts and suppression of the pesents.*
## Terrain & Geography
To the north of Kronharth lies [[Embeth]] and [[Mivon]], to its west and south lays ocean and to the east lies mountains.

Kronharth has mostly a quite mild climate with seasons that allow for large growing periods. There are many lakes and rivers and a few forested areas. The mild climate allows the region for many orchards which often liter the hilly landscapes.
## Economy
**Resources:** Wine, Vegetables, Fruits, Fish, Squid, Mercenaries, Glass
**Technology:** Medieval
## Politics
The Divine Imperium of Kronharth is ruled by the Imperial Emperor but they have very little power in practice . Local nobles that often have titles of princes and kings hold a lot of power locally and regularly wage war against one another.

When an emperor passes away a new empire is elected by the nobles who have earned the title of king but it is almost always the [[Von Hohenmark family]] that is elected in practice.

The class structure in Kronharth is strong and it is difficult to advance beyond your station in life. Peasants hold a notably lower status than their ruling nobles, in practice their almost their property.
## Culture
Kronharth is dominated by romantic notions of chivalry and knighthood. This means those who are strong should protect the weak and everyone owes fealty to their lord. However in practice these things are often flexible to suit the needs of the one who seeks it.

Questing knights and holy pilgrims hold special statuses and generally any lord or citizen is ment to aid them in these endeavors. Arcane magic is generally seen as having a lower status in Kronharth than martial prowess and divine magic.

Merchants and people without roots in the land generally hold a notable lower status in the lands.
## Faith
**Notable Deities:** [[Sandor]], [[Vanee]], [[Xiander]]
**Disliked Deities:** [[Bann]], [[Rendeen]], [[Thirdenlin]]
## Relations
**[[Mivon]]:** Mivon is a revolter nation that broke free from Kronharth during the [[Mivon independence conflict]] and Kronharth has never accepted their independence leading to tense relations.
## History
**Founding History:** Kronharth is said to have been founded in ancient times and little is known of how the empire originally came about.

**Recent History:** 
- The [[Period of three Emperors]] and the instability it wrought including the [[Mivon independence conflict]] has left the empire even more weak and divided than typical. Much effort has been put towards unifying and recovering from this rocky period.
