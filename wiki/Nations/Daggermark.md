# Daggermark
**Capital:** Suzerain

**Common Races:** Changelings, Drow, Duergar, Kenkus
**Uncommon Races:** Bugbears, Centaurs, Half-Elves, Humans, Tieflings

*Daggermark is a nation which most avoid. It is very isolated and known for its slavery practices and living sacrifices. Daggermark does however posses skilled hexers, assassins and spies. Daggermark is generally seen as teetering on the [[Sleeping Realm]].*
## Terrain & Geography
North of Daggermark are the [[Manchu Tribes]] with hostile lands separating the nations. To the east over the ocean is [[Gralton]]. To the west and south of Daggermark is vast oceans.

Most of Daggermark consist of jagged peaks and ashen deserts surrounding them, the northern parts hold more swamps and dense woods. The coasts of Daggermark is littered by sharp islands and with torturous beaches. The days in Daggermark are unbearable hot and the nights cold. 

The magic in Daggermark is constantly unstable affecting all arcane casting making it powerful but dangerous to use. Furthermore so called arcane storms are common.
## Economy
**Resources:** Assassins, Poisons, Chemicals, Obsidian, Silk, Slaves
**Technology:** Medieval with especially advanced poison and torturing devices
## Politics
The nation of Daggermark is led by the Matriarch a woman who is chosen by [[Ruzhee]] as. The matriarch is all powerful and to question her decisions is the same as questioning the goddess and therefore can warrant a death penalty. The current matriarch is called [[Jerrezia Stillborn]] and is known to be cunning, secretive and ruthless.

Society in Daggermark has diufferent divisions that affect all of society.
- Drow - The ruler class, administrative, religious and warrior duties. Female drow generally have a higher status than men.
- Changelings & Kenkus - The high end worker class often working with spying, assassinations, trading and other important duties.
- Duergar - The low end worker class working mostly with mining, building and other physical labor.
- Slaves - All other races are subservient and property, they have little protection and are often worked to death or experimented on.
## Culture
Daggermarks culture is dominated by a sense of hatred to the outside world and its inhabitants. This hatred contributes to the slavery and utter indifference to the slaves and their life. The hatred runs especially deep towards [[Dwarven Keeps]].

Culture in Daggermark concentrates on the Drow, slavery and the goddess [[Ruzhee]]. All members of the Drow are ment to pray to the Goddess every year. An important coming of age ceremony for the drow is torturing a person (usually a slave) and sacrificing them in the name of the goddess.
## Faith
**Notable Deities:** [[Bann]], [[Ruzhee]], [[Xiander]]
**Disliked Deities:** [[Dunden]], [[Rendeen]], [[Vanee]]
## Relations
**[[Pitax]]:** [[Pitax]] is the only nation who has warm relations with Daggermark. The two nations have at times aided each other in crises and they trade resources occasionally.

**[[Gralton]]:** [[Gralton]] is except for [[Pitax]] the only nation allowed to conduct trade with Daggermark. All trade is however strictly limited to the port of [[Nerrez]].
## History
**Founding History:** The modern nation of Daggermark was created at the end of the [[War of the beards]] with the [[Devastation of Daggermark]] which annihilated the nation of Daggermark and let it rise anew from the ashes.
