# Troll Hunt (unfinished)
## Chapter 1 
[[Kesten Garess]]n tightened his grip around his blade, the hilt felt firm and reassuring in his grasp. The entire blade was a reminder of his duty, the sword called the Ernest blade had been a gift from [[Jamandi Aldori]] herself for his long service to her house. Right now the blade gave him the confidence needed to continue this expedition he had been sent on by his new charge. 

In front of him walked a petite woman in gray garb, Sylthana better known as [[Siphon]] was a competent scout and skilled archer. Right now Siphon seemed to struggle between keeping watch and thinking of way to play tricks on a friend back home. *Those sneezing flowers you mentioned earlier, do you think we will find any? I think they would work perfectly for a plan involving [[Crash]]* Siphon said with a gleam in her eye.

Beside the gray clad girl walked a more scruffy man in leather clothing with several tools and bags dangling in his belt. The man answered quietly *I shall keep an eye out for them*. [[Siphon]] quickly replied with a grin *Thank you!* . The man called [[Brunno Meadowmane]] was a forester by trade and rarely spoke much or for to long.

From behind Kesten heard an obviously bored voice *Are we gonna fight something soon? At this rate boredom may kill me before any foe has a chance*. Making up the rearguard of the group walked a large thiefling with long horns covered in metal and furs. [[Velmine]] wielding her halberd was a terrifying foe for any opponent, but she was a person rather more focused on battle than travel.

*Pace yourself, we don not know if the reports are even true.* Kesten responded in a neutral manor. In the middle of the group walked Kesten himself a man nearing his 50s with some leather and metal armor of his own. Kesten had been charged by the baronesses to investigate reports of trolls in the area. Kesten himself had put together this group of colorful characters, while they all had their quirks Kesten were confident in their abilities. Siphon and Velmine were both plenty battle proven and Brunno knew these forests better than anyone else in [[Summerborn]].

*Brunno, do you think we will reach the site today?* Kesten asked plainly. Brunno squinted at the sky and though for a moment. *We should arrive at the river by early evening at this rate* he answered in a low tone. *In that case I think we should put up camp at the next appropriate site. I do not want to encounter a troll in the dark.* Kesten said to the group. 

*Wait, I can feel my horns itching, something aint right here.* Velmine said as she stopped in her tracks. *Now that you mention it there is no singing amongst the birds* Brunno said while closing his eyes and listening. Kesten looked around at the trees and suddenly felt more exposed than he would have ever hoped for, trolls were not known for ambushing tactics but there were plenty of other things around these parts that could be dangerous.

Kesten took a deep breath before quickly giving out orders *Siphon, get a vantage point and look around if you can see anything. Velmine keep your eyes and ears sharp for anything out of the ordinary. Brunno, be ready to take cover*. Velmine and Kesten quickly stod back to back while Siphon deftly climbed a nearby tree and Brunno gripped his axe. 

After a few minutes of complete silence Siphon called out from the tree top *I can see a clearing, with a wagon, I think there's people there*. Kesten considered this for a moment before responding *Let us make our way there carefully, maybe they know somethign about any trolls in the area. Be on your guard though, there are few people with honorable intentions in these parts of the [[Stolen Lands]]*.

The group formed up and head into the tree line.
## Chapter 2
The trees in this region were tall and cast shadows dancing on the ground and the ground cover made plenty of noises as they walked along. To [[Velmine]] this was reassuring, if they had such a hard time moving quietly any opponent would be sure to have the same issue. Velmine could never quite explain how or why something was wrong, it was just a feeling in her gut. But her gut hadn't led her astray through her many years of mercenary work, she had learned to trust her instincts.

After another few minutes of walking she felt it, the unmistakable signs of battle. She felt the scent of blood in the air, the quiet of the surroundings and the taste of iron in her mouth. Velmine spoke up in a whisper *There's been a battle here, based on the silence it is already concluded*. [[Kesten Garess]] and [[Brunno Meadowmane]] turned to Velmine a bit puzzled over the statement. As they stopped for a moment they heard [[Siphon]] from the front *I see it, it seems calm now but, there's a lot of bodies*.

The scene before them was more something Velmine would describe as a slaughter rather than a battle. Four people clad in tatters and a mule lay on the ground, covered in deep gashes and one with limbs jutting in unnatural directions. Beside them a small wagon with a few crates seemingly untouched.

Velmine walked forth and took in the sight, after many battles she could almost see the scene playing out, some unstoppable force walking in and killing these smugglers with little difficulty. Velmine could however not help but be impressed by these smugglers, it takes some serious bravery to look death in the eye and stand their ground like these smugglers have. Either that or they had been frozen with fear.

Velmine knelled down beside a body and tasted a finger of blood from one of the corpses. With her companions staring at her bewildered she took to word *I'd say two to three hours since they died*.

After a few moments of silence Kesten spoke *Looking at the battle scars on the people it was clearly not a person who did this, and whatever did it did not steal these peoples supplies. I would say there is a possibility that this is the troll we are looking for*. Another moment of silence until Siphon asked what everyone was thinking *what do we do know?*
## Chapter 3
Looking at the newly dug shallow graves [[Brunno Meadowmane]] could not stop his hands shaking. Surrounded by these hardened warriors Brunno had a hard time taking in how numbed they seemingly were to the death of these people. 

The group started turning to [[Kesten Garess]] anticipating what he would say next. Kesten words where however the last hing Brunno wanted to hear *Brunno can you track this thing?*. Brunno heard his answer all though it felt like it was some else who said it *Shouldn't be much different than tracking a bear*.

Kesten quickly looked at [[Siphon]] and [[Velmine]] before calmly and confidently choosing his new words. *I want to be sure this is a troll and if possible kill it. Ready your weapons, no necessary talking beyond this point.*

Brunnos expert eyes quickly found the trail and saw its large feet imprints in a piece of mud and several broken branches. Following the trail Brunno listened intently and couldn't shake the feeling that something was off.

It nagged at Brunno for the next hour of tracking, it was far more difficult than expected. With that thought Brunno realized what was bugging him, the troll was hiding its tracks, it seemed to avoid steeping in soft flooring and avoided breaking the shrubbery and bushes. Having heard the descriptions from the court mage [[Bille Frostbite]] the trolls shouldn't be smart enough to camouflage the tracks.

After another two hours Brunno saw the sun starting to close on the horizon, Brunno expected maybe two more hours before the darkness would make further tracking impossible. But right now the tracks were very obvious, the troll seemed to have broken every branch in the area and have left almost ridiculously deep footprints at points. 

Brunno mumbled to himself *why the change?* It dawned on Brunno just barley in time as he quickly turned around, *this is a trap*. Just as he said that he could see a large figure starting to move a few meters from their position towards them. The troll had walked in a circle to make an ambush! *Over there* Brunno shouted and pointed while taking several steps back.
## Chapter 4
[[Siphon]] had never been a brave person, but there certainly wasn't anything wrong with her reflexes. By the times she had turned around she had her shortbow in her hands. Before she even had time to think she had an arrow on the string and within another second it was soaring through the air.

Siphon could immediately tell it would hit and now took a second to scan her surroundings. [[Brunno Meadowmane]] was running for cover behind her. To Siphons left she could see [[Kesten Garess]] raising his blade, his eyes darting around. Running past her she could see [[Velmine]] charging the intruder with long strides. As Siphon turned back her attention she saw the arrow had hit slightly above the trolls eye letting blood triple down its face and making it stumble as it charged.

She heard Kesten below orders *There's no time to oil the weapons! Siphon use the branded arrows, me and Velmine will get to work!* Right after Kesten finished the order Velmine connected with the troll horns straight in to the guts of the troll making it shout in an unknown guttering language *Rummba, keashn rai!*

Siphon could see the threat she had been told about, rather than buckling under the damage the troll stod firm and the arrow fell out as the wound closed in its forehead. The troll reached out with its hand and grabbed Velmine at one of her horns with its inhuman strength. 

Siphon fired two flame arrows in rapid succession each hitting the trolls chest a few centimeters apart. The arrows that been branded by [[Annj Bearer]] lit up with embers that seemed to dance over its skin. Rather than hurt the troll though the troll gave a guttural laugh as it lifted Velmine by the horn.

Velmine drove her halbered deep in the trolls guts and Kesten took his blade deep in to its arm. The wounds clearly hurt the troll as it screamed in anger, but the damage kept repairing. The troll almost ignored Kesten as its other hand hit Velmine with such strong force that she flew several meters into a tree her horn broken with it in the trolls hand.

Kesten stod and forced himself to take a deep breath as the troll turned towards him. he swung his blade once more hitting the arm that was now hanging limply at the trolls side. However to late Kesten saw the claws coming towards him from above. A fraction before they hit an arrow hit the troll in the eye making the devastating hit only scratch Kestens face.

With one eye out of operation Kesten shouted a new *the flames aren't working! Retreat, I'll cover you.* Velmine stod up with a few broken ribs, every breath sending pain through her body. Velmine did the only thing she could, she ignored the order and ran to Kestens aid Halberd driving deep into the trolls shoulder blade. She yelled at the top of her lungs *flames or not it still bleeds!*
