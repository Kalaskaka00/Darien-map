# Kingmaker
## Act 1 - The Stolen Lands
**Session 1:** *Assault on Aldori Mansion*
A group of adventurers and mercenaries gather at [[Jamandi Aldori]]s request on the quest to tame the [[Stolen Lands]] with riches and noble titles promised to those who succeed. The [[Vaegarian host]] head out directly in the evening while the rest wait until the morning.

During the night the mansion is assaulted by mercenaries seemingly from [[Pitax]] from kill many adventurers and staff in their sleep. [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] join forces with [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]], [[Brave Wish]] and [[Velmine]] to clear out the threats.

As the threats come to an end Tandeen frames the party and refuses to travel with them. Leading to [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]], [[Brave Wish]] and [[Velmine]] being sent away in one party and [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] in another.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Jamandi Aldori]], [[Maegar Varn]], [[Cephal Lorentus]], [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]], [[Brave Wish]], [[Velmine]], [[Kesten Garess]], [[Kassil Aldori]]

**Session 2:** *Entry into the Stolen Lands*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] arrived at [[Oleg's Outpost]] in time to send away some bandits that were trying to extort the outpost. The bandits claimed to be working for the [[Stag Lord]] and that this would be regretted.

The bandits reappeared led by their lieutenant [[Crash]]. The protagonists managed to defeat the bandits and capture them and their lieutenant. 

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Oleg Crank]], [[Gildi Crank]], [[Sparrow]], [[Crash]]

**Session 3:** *Drug run*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] managed to convince [[Crash]] to join their side and guard [[Oleg's Outpost]] and released the other thugs. 

[[Sparrow]] requested aid in getting some academical supplies for his medicine. The protagonists encountered some water faeries and scraped some bones from a very old  skeletal monster.

**Session 4:** *Boar Trouble*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] agreed on [[Oleg Crank]]s quest to chase of a big boar that was scaring merchants. The tam dealt with the wrathful dire boar and avoided killings its underlings.

**Session 5:** *Stag Home*
A dryad named [[Nentrah]] appears before [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] and tells the protagonists that they can acquire an important artifact but they must hurry before the [[Stag Lord]]s men arrive.

The protagonists sets out and meet two peculiar characters on the way called [[Zara]] and [[Shrowm]].

The protagonists arrive at the [[Stag Lord]]s old home and start the search. Soon after the search [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]], [[Brave Wish]] and [[Velmine]] arrive as well as the [[Stag Lord]]s men led by [[Ox]] and aided by [[Siphon]]. 

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Nentrah]], [[Zara]], [[Shrowm]], [[Ox]], [[Siphon]]

**Session 6:** *The Seed*
The fight was intense but [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] successfully acquired the seed and won the battle while their foes escape. The protagonists also manage to capture [[Siphon]] and convince her to return with them to [[Oleg's Outpost]] with [[Crash]]. 

Casualties: [[Velmine]] was seriously wounded in the battle.

**Session 7:** *New symbols*
After the battle [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] finished looking around the area and found a tree with an odd unfamiliar symbol on it. The tree was clearly magical and seemingly cursed, but the protagonists lacked means to deal with it.

After returning to [[Oleg's Outpost]] [[Siphon]] and [[Crash]] had a heartwarming reunion. At the outpost [[Kesten Garess]] appeared with some [[Aldori family]] guards to arrest [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]] as investigations had shown him to be a spy working for [[Pitax]].

**Session 8:** *The Cursed Lands*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] headed south to investigate and find [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]]. 

When heading south the group encountered [[Tiv]] a paladin from [[Mivon]] investigating suspected kidnappings in the [[Stolen Lands]]. [[Tiv]] was however cursed to be locked in constant battle getting slowly worn down. The protagonists were unable to break said curse but managed to help [[Tiv]] rest and gave her some supplies.

Continuing south the group also saw a dead unicorn close to a dangerous wood clearly radiating an ominous aura. 

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Tiv]]

**Session 9:** *Ambushers*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] was led to [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]], [[Brave Wish]] and [[Velmine]] and successfully ambushed and slew [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]] and captured [[Brave Wish]] and [[Velmine]] whom clearly had had their minds altered by [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]].

When traveling back [[Ox]] tried or take his revenge with the aid of the [[Stag Lord]]s other two lieutenants [[Bille Frostbite]] and [[Xer]]. The group managed to capture [[Ox]] and [[Bille Frostbite]] but found [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]]s body to be missing.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Bille Frostbite]], [[Xer]]
Casualties: [[Xer]] was quickly struck down by a might strike from [[Bree Caley]] after planting a kiss on her.

**Session 10:** *New alliances*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] successfully helped rehabilitate [[Brave Wish]] and [[Velmine]]. Furthermore the protagonists managed to create a temporary alliance with [[Ox]] and [[Bille Frostbite]].

Gathering their forces the protagonists set of south. On their way they had finally acquired to power needed to free [[Tiv]] from her curse, [[Tiv]] agreed to in return help the protagonists with defeating the bandits.

**Session 11:** *Stag Keep*
The party led by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] arrived at Stag Keep and started studying its defenses. Before the battle commenced [[Nentrah]] asked to acquire the seed in order to keep it safe, a request which [[Claire Nightshield]] refused, this clearly angered their dryad ally. 

**Session 12:** *The battle of Stag Keep*
The attack began with [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]], [[Claire Nightshield]] and [[Ox]]attacking and distracting from the front, quickly getting into a fight with the [[Stag Lord]] himself.

Meanwhile [[Kesten Garess]], [[Bille Frostbite]] and [[Velmine]] sneaked in from the back to create panic in the camp.

On another front [[Tiv]], [[Brave Wish]], [[Crash]] and [[Siphon]] sneaked in to secure the menagerie and stop it from reinforcing the [[Stag Lord]].

The main battle was bloody and tough but ended with the [[Stag Lord]]s defeat and the surrender of the remaining bandits. [[Yelgolor Remoudou]] and [[Zara]] came in as unexpected reinforcements during the battle.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Stag Lord]], [[Yelgolor Remoudou]]
Casualties: The [[Stag Lord]] and [[Ox]] killed each other in the battle. Two boars gave their life for the cause.
## Act 2 - A new barony
**Session 13:** *Baronesses*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] were invited to [[Restov]] for a ceremony. During the ceremony they were made into baronesses of the [[Stolen Lands]] and [[Maegar Varn]] was made into baron of [[Varnhold]].

The protagonists chose [[Magrie Beeret]] to be a representative of [[Brevoy]] in the new barony. [[Kesten Garess]] also chose to go with the newly appointed baronesses and end his service with the [[Aldori family]].

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Iandri Flukeflow]], [[Rita Neat]], [[Keldithas Beeret]], [[Magrie Beeret]], [[Kiri Udoon]], [[Maameo Udoon]], [[Thorin Udoon]], [[Calsys Viine]], [[Sandak Viine]]

**Session 14:** *New Settlers*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] spoke to some of the settlers who would go to [[Summerborn]] and escorted them.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Brunno Meadowmane]], [[Garbin Sunheart]], [[Adsatra Sagebrook]], [[Movh Bearer]], [[Annj Bearer]], [[Drift]]

**Session 15:** *Kidnapped Children*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] agreed to help [[Tiv]] and [[Jonas Arc]] in finding the kidnapped children from [[Mivon]] heading to the swamps in their investigation.

They found a bandit camp and made a plan of attack. 

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Jonas Arc]]

**Session 16:** *The Bandit Camp*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] sneaked in with [[Jonas Arc]] and [[Tiv]] and found the children. They fought their way out killing and capturing most bandits, however the bandit leader [[Ura Clawt]] managed to get away.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Ura Clawt]]

**Session 17:** *A Barony*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] arrived back to [[Summerborn]] and saw of the [[Mivon]] expedition. When arriving back three new settlers had arrived and with some promotions a full council was completed for the [[Golden Lands]]:
- Regent [[Karri]]
- General [[Kesten Garess]]
- Treasurer [[Magrie Beeret]]
- High Priest [[Drift]]
- Grand Diplomat [[Pixana Boldshard]]

The protagonists traveled back to [[Oleg's Outpost]] towards Stag Home. On the way they made acquaintances with [[Magie]] and [[Laura Faelamin]]. They also came to see a newly sober and sick [[Sparrow]].

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Chirp]], [[Pixana Boldshard]], [[Karri]], [[Magie]], [[Laura Faelamin]]

**Session 18:** *Betrayal*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] where quickly attacked by [[Nentrah]] after at arriving at Stag Home in order to acquire the Seed. The fight was tough but the protagonists were successful, after some unexpected aid from [[Harlequin]], [[Joker]] and [[Mime]]. 

After the battle was over the protagonists freed [[Myron]] from the [[Stag Lord]]s necklace and her familiar Oak from the cursed tree. 

The protagonists also encountered a traveler on the way back home.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Myron]], [[Peck Arck]]

**Session 19:** *Troll Troubles*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] arrived back in [[Summerborn]] to encounter a new traveler named [[Magpie]]. 

Soon after arriving back the protagonists were encountered with a failed expedition which led to a seriously wounded [[Kesten Garess]] and [[Velmine]] brought back by [[Siphon]] and [[Brunno Meadowmane]]. [[Kesten Garess]] warned about especially dangerous trolls.

The protagonists set out immediately to deal with the issue and were quickly ambushed by trolls and saved by a small grung who jump of of nowhere and mentioned they had the same quest.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Magpie]], [[n'Neb]]

**Session 20:** *The Troll King*
After following the trolls to [[Stone Road]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]], [[Claire Nightshield]] and [[n'Neb]] were introduced to [[Chorrad Troll King]] and his advisor [[Tandeen Dazzlehand|Marionette]]. A fight quickly broke out and the foes were rapidly defeated.

The advisor turned out to be some morph
ed form of [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]] seemingly unexplained. The remaining trolls quickly lost any cohesion and [[Stone Road]] was once more abandoned.

[[n'Neb]] agreed to travel back to [[Summerborn]] with the protagonists as they encountered an unexpected bard in the wilds and his deer.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Chorrad Troll King]], [[Tandeen Dazzlehand|Marionette]], [[Allas Dawnd]]
Casualties: [[Chorrad Troll King]], [[Tandeen Dazzlehand]] (again)

**Session 21 & 22:** *A plea for help*
Heading back to [[Summerborn]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] recruited [[n'Neb]] to join them. 

Arriving back to [[Summerborn]] they found a letter from [[Maegar Varn]] claiming that [[Varnhold]]s food stores for the winter were in dire straights, and he offered payment in kind. The protagonists quickly decided to gather their spare provision and head out with [[Bille Frostbite]], [[Annj Bearer]], [[Milo Grainseller]] and [[Garbin Sunheart]] in toe.

On the way to [[Varnhold]] they also encountered a traveling artist who asked to travel with them.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Reina Palett]]

**Session 23:** *Welcome to Varnhold*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] arrived in [[Varnhold]] and were quickly greeted by [[Maegar Varn]] and [[Cephal Lorentus]] and their council [[Malora Grainseller]] and [[Zayne Copper]], The Grainsellers quickly went into negotiating for the delivery.

Meanwhile [[Bree Caley]] discovered an old mentor of hers [[Vondal Sandwashed]] who had moved from [[The Wastes]].

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Malora Grainseller]], [[Zayne Copper]], [[Vondal Sandwashed]]

**Session 24:** *Travelers*
Returning to [[Summerborn]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] significantly lessened [[Zara]]s curse of lycanthropy and gave her a greater degree of control. 

When arriving back in [[Summerborn]] they also encountered two "new" faces to the town, a traveling daughter of the [[Viine family]] and a shady fella from [[Pitax]].

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Emily Viine]], [[Wit]]

**Session 25 & 26:** *The curse of Candelmore*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] decided to travel towards the ruins of [[Candelmore]] to figure out rumors going around. While traveling their ship was quickly attacked which led to passing out.

The protagonists woke up on a foreign island and quickly encountered five of the [[Candle Mages]] whom seemed alive but odd and forgetful. The protagonists quickly realized a curse was about and affected. The protagonists managed to find a way to break the curse without having all the [[Candle Mages]] die in the process and freed them.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Flor Energa]], [[Ihara Absul]], [[Whirl Palett]], [[Deren Seere]], [[Yarslav Crain]]

**Session 27 & 28:** *A war on several fronts*
Arriving back in [[Summerborn]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] were quickly made aware of several crises acting on at once:
- Supplies being stolen from [[Summerborn]]
- [[Milo Grainseller]], [[Chirp]] and [[Garbin Sunheart]] acting out of charachter
- [[Oleg's Outpost]] under siege from lizardfolks
- A missing patrol including [[Siphon]] going missing near the eastern mountain range

In investigating this [[Milo Grainseller]], [[Chirp]] and [[Garbin Sunheart]] were kidnapped by some doppelgangers, in trying to stop them [[Chirp]] almost died and [[Milo Grainseller]] proposed to [[Bree Caley]].

A dragonborn called [[Egizaed]] offered to help talk down the lizard-folks at [[Oleg's Outpost]] and [[Myron]] offered to do so in exchange of a favor further on.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Egizaed]]

**Session 29 & 30:** *Pitaxian ruins*
Traveling to the mountains in the east of the [[Stolen Lands]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] found [[Siphon]] caring for a wounded [[Brunno Meadowmane]]. Their were apparently monster infested ruins in the mountain.

The protagonists ventured inside and found that the ruins were [[Pitax]]ian in origin. After dispatching several monsters the protagonists found a minor reflection of the [[Dream Realm]], a journal, some treasure and most importantly a highly charged [[Fluix Crystal]].

Satisfied with the immediate threat of the monsters being taken care of the protagonists ventured onward to aid [[Oleg's Outpost]].

**Session 31:** *The Siege of Olegs Outpost*
Arriving at [[Oleg's Outpost]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] found an ongoing siege. Going into mediation with the lizardfolk it became clear that [[Nentrah]] hade egged them on. 

With [[Egizaed]] as a mediator the protagonists managed to find an amicable agreement.

When talking with the lizardfolks it became clear that [[Myron]] hade become captured by [[Nentrah]].

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Vacho]], [[Aelthot]], [[Balrotho]]

**Session 32:** *Unwelcome encounters*
Arriving back in [[Summerborn]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] had to deal with a seriously agitated [[Pixana Boldshard]] whom blamed them putting [[Chirp]] in danger during the kidnapping.

Furthermore a delegation from [[Mivon]] arrived where one member clearly agitated over [[Silvana Ashford]]s history and dealing with this led to an all in all poor reception of the envoys.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Rein Stagler]], [[Barnur Keenhold]]

**Session 33 & 34:** *Defusing a bomb and the fall of a hero*
Going back to the [[Pitax]]ian ruins [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] with the aid of [[Annj Bearer]], [[Maegar Varn]] and [[Cephal Lorentus]] went to deal with the big magic build up in the [[Fluix Crystal]].

Arriving they came to find [[Nentrah]] and a fight quickly broke out. As [[Nentrah]] tried to flee [[Claire Nightshield]] tried to dive after her and followed her through a portal by an unfortunate turn of events. 

After several agonizing moments [[Claire Nightshield]] came back dying and speaking of someone who stod above [[Nentrah]] a person emanating power and with a dreadfull appearance. [[Claire Nightshield]] passed away thereafter.

Casualties: [[Claire Nightshield]]
## Act 3 - Dangers in all directions
**Session 35, 36 & 37:** *The Varnhold Vanishing*
A few weeks after the death of [[Claire Nightshield]] [[Silvana Ashford]] and [[Bree Caley]] keep dealign with the events of the [[Golden Lands]] and are finally getting a handle on things. Just when thigns seem in order a small quickling named [[Conleth Umbra]] comes running claiming that something horrible has happened in [[Varnhold]].

With that hesitance our protagonists set out to [[Varnhold]] to aid their ally immediately. They quickly find the town entirely abondened with few traces. The protagonists decide to look into the centaur tribes who've repeatedly fought with [[Varnhold]] to find they suffer the samme issues. A centaur shaman goes as far as to give a blessing to the protagonists in hope of them freeing the centaurs as well.

Keeping on the tracking it leads to ruins and a terrified [[Peck Arck]]. Peck explains that he accidentally awakened some ancient evil who seems to have control over the entire area. After a fight they had back towards [[Summerborn]] to regroup and make a plan.

While heading back they also encounter an abandoned warforged whom they get up and running it was apparently one of the missing [[Candle Mages]] whom almost immediately cures [[Magie]]s curse bring the amount of [[Candle Mages]] from 5 out of 8 to 7 out of 8.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Dwen'zur Raincleanser]], [[Daedove Wildguard]], [[Derk]]

**Session 38 & 39:** *Thundaraks reign*
Delving into the ruins with a protective ward of [[Derk]] and a minor backup of [[Peck Arck]], [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] fought several mind controlled puppets of an undead ruler called [[Thundarak]].

After defeating amongst others [[Maegar Varn]], [[Cephal Lorentus]] and a dragon they arrived to find the undead cyclops known as [[Thundarak]] who apparently possessed [[Divine Artifacts|the eye of Xiander]] they managed to defeat him after a difficult fight only to have the relic taken by [[Harlequin]], [[Joker]] and [[Mime]] in exchange for the [[Lord of Stories]] promising not protect [[Nentrah]].

After being freed [[Maegar Varn]] suggested a union between the [[Golden Lands]] and [[Varnhold]] where Maegar offered to be the junior partner.

**Session 40, 41 & 42:** *Curses, mists and democracy*
After a rest in [[Summerborn]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] woke up to find themselves cursed and [[Silvana Ashford]] had an inkling it had to do with her "family". The protagonists set out to find the cause and deal with their curses.

Soon after heading out they ran into a group of commoners from [[Varnhold]] led by [[Errard Specher]] who demanded democratic reforms. The protagonists sent them to leave their opinions to the council.

After a long search for the source and walking through some ominous mists the protagonists found them selves at the village of [[Serenity]] and its odd inhabitants. The protagonists helped to defeat a sea monster that turned out to be a naga of a type that is typically friendly.

The protagonists later found [[Beheria]] and [[Oliveria]] who confirmed they were responsible for the curses and agreed to remove them. The hags and protagonists made a deal where the hags agreed to tell them any information they knew and to give them a ward against curses, in return the protagonists were to leave [[Serenity]] untouched and help [[Wyndee]] regain her power.

The hags told the protagonists a few pieces of information:
- The [[Lord of Stories]] has stolen power of curses from [[Wyndee]].
- The [[Lord of Stories]] made a deal with [[Nentrah]] long ago and the seed is her heart.
- They told any background information they had about the Jesters.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Errard Specher]], [[Artarp Rein]], [[Ninne]], [[Odak One Eye]], [[Yarek Gray]], [[Beheria]], [[Oliveria]]

**Session 43:** *The Crowns oversight*
Arriving back to [[Summerborn]] [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] found [[Rita Neat]] present and immediately putting her nose into their business. She both introduced taxes on the Barony, asked the baronesses to clamp down on the democracy movement and to reorganise the council.

**Session 44 & 45:** *Assault on Summerborn*
[[Nentrah]] and her army descended upon [[Summerborn]] while [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] defended it together with all who were available. The battle luckily resulted in minimal losesses with only [[Kesten Garess]], [[Bille Frostbite]] and some militia suffered significant injuries.

[[Nentrah]] was captured and brought to the most secure cell available.

**Session 46:** *Interrogations and Negotiations*
With [[Nentrah]] captured [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] began interrogating her. They were unable to convince [[Nentrah]] to let go of significant information but were able to confirm [[Myron]]s health and get an idea of her whereabouts. 

Soon after [[Rita Neat]] and [[Reina Palett]] departed a delegation from [[Pitax]] arrived to discuss a secret pact of friendship. After some quite quick discussions they ended with a pact that give [[Pitax]] a claim to part of the [[Stolen Lands]] and made a defensive pact between the parties, [[Pitax]] also agreed to send over some information about their previous research in the [[Stolen Lands]].

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Farlamin Mistshaper]], [[Lalne Quill]]

**Session 47:** *The Delivery*
[[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Conleth Umbra]] received [[Turtahir]], [[Meel'a]] and [[Agamoos Evercrown]] from [[Vital Delivery]]. They got a key to the Devils bargain where they got some exceptional items reserved from [[Maegar Varn]].

[[Joker]], [[Harlequin]] and [[Mime]] came and informed the protagonists that their reckoning is upcoming. 

A firework festival started celebrating the deal with [[Pitax]] where [[Farlamin Mistshaper]] put on some magical fireworks.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Agamoos Evercrown]], [[Turtahir]], [[Meel'a]], [[Ichor]], [[Cypher]]
