# Vital Delivery
**Session 1:** *A mysterious cargo*
[[Turtahir]], [[Meel'a]] and [[Agamoos Evercrown]] got a mission from [[Jarlek Grin]] to travel to the baronesses of [[Summerborn]] and deliver a cargo that was secret but important. The group borrowed a wagon, got some fake cargo and set out.

Soon after setting out at the city gate one of [[Jarlek Grin]]s rivals tried to stop the protagonists but they managed through some clever maneuvering to make their way out and set of on the way.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Jarlek Grin]], [[Asha Rakker]]

**Session 2:** *Undercover*
Soon after arriving in [[Floureton]] [[Asha Rakker]] strikes again cornering [[Turtahir]], [[Meel'a]] and [[Agamoos Evercrown]] and trying to negotiate a settlement. When the negotiations fail she threatens to use a mercenary band to get her way with the help of [[Heren Blaze]]. The protagonists change wagon and escape unnoticed.

The protagonists dart through [[Touvette]] only to encounter some incredible dangerous cooking.

Eventually arriving at [[Pitax]] they count themselves lucky as the border guard was an old servant of [[Agamoos Evercrown]] who helps them pass by under the radar.

Newly Introduced NPCs: [[Heren Blaze]], [[Joanna Tang]]

**Session 3:** *Rapid delivery*
Arriving at [[Great Gate]] [[Turtahir]], [[Meel'a]] and [[Agamoos Evercrown]] were forced to find a way around through the mountain caves. After a tough trip through the caves they were immediate faced by a blizzard in [[Numeria]] witch they got shelter from.

Soon after the blizzard subsided [[Heren Blaze]] and her gang caught up to the protagonists setting the scene for a chase which the protagonists drove at break neck speed eventually leaving [[Heren Blaze]] and [[Asha Rakker]] in the dust.

The protagonists arrived at [[Oleg's Outpost]] and spoke with [[Oleg Crank]], they only paused for a brief resupply before going on at the break neck speed. The protagonists were assaulted by flickerflies and only got through the battle with the grit of their teeth. After a short recovery they arrived at [[Summerborn]] to join the [[Kingmaker]] campaign.
