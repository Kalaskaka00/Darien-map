# Rare Materials
## Arcane Materials
**Fluix Crystals**
Flux Crystal seems to be a type of mineral that mostly forms around pathways to other realms. They are white tinted crystals that absorb magical energy from spells and the surrounding area.

They have at times been researched but are still to this day rarely used, booth because they are rare and volatile.
*[[Annj Bearer]] {{year:3864}}*

**Stardust**
A magical material called Stardust which they sell to mages, alchemists and astrologers.
## Metals
**Adamantine**
Adamantine is one of the hardest and sturdiest metals one can find. It does however have two significant drawbacks, one is its weight making it unwieldy and secondly is how hard it is to work.

Most adamantine used is produced in [[Iceless Deep]] which one of few places that has the metal and has the forges and smithies that can work it.
*[[Bralon Alepike]] {{year:3845}}*

**Shear Coral**
Shear Coral is a material especially known for being able to withstand very high and low temperatures and for its icy glossy look. Its produced in [[Rough Loch]] and is mostly used in armor, structures, tools and art.
