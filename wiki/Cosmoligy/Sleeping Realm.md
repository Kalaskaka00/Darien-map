The Sleeping realm is a part of the [[Material Plane]]. The Sleeping Realm is known to be gray, quiet and mostly empty. 

The Sleeping realm is usually described as the opposite of the [[Dream Realm]] and a reflection of the [[Waking World]]. The Sleeping realm is shaped by missed opportunities, regrets and what could have been, this means the realm is often desolate and depressing.

The Sleeping Realm is also known as the Shadowfell, Land of darkness and World of Gray. The lans are mostly inhabited by fey creatures.
