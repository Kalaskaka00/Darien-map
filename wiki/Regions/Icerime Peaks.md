# Icerime Peaks
*Icerime Peaks are amongst the most challenging and towering mountains in the region.*
## Geography
The Icerime Peaks lays towards the [[Frozen Sea]] between [[Brevoy]] and [[The Wastes]] creating a natural border.

The peaks are tall and are known to be challenging to traverse as the mountains are windy and can swing in temperatures wildly. The constant temperature shifts makes the ice especially tricky and unpredictable.
