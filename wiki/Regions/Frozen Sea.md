# Frozen Sea
*The Frozen sea is an incredible cold place in various degrees of frozen over through out the year.*
## Geography
The Frozen Sea lay north of [[Brevoy]] and [[Numeria]] booth of which have few settlements along the coast.

The frozen sea is almost always frozen over, but during the warmer months it is possible to break the ice and during the colder months it is very difficult to traverse.
