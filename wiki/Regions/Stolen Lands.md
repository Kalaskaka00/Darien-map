# Stolen Lands
*The Stolen Lands is a region that has been known for its dangerous wilderness and closeness to the [[Dream Realm]]*
## Geography
The Stolen Lands lay in the [[Golden Lands]] between [[Embeth]] and [[Brevoy]] and borders the [[Nalmarches]]. In the center and most prominent in the Stolen Lands lay [[Summerborn]] and the [[World Tree]].

It is known for its quite plain terrain with winds drawing through and several rivers meandering the area. Inside the area lays a few quite dense forests
## History
The Stolen Lands where for a long time a home of the [[Dream Realm]] until around Year {{year:2600}} when the area was slowly decimated. After this time many groups would rise and fall in the area including:
  - {{year:2900}} A crusader state devastated during the [[War of the beards]].
  - {{year:3100}} Giant lands led by [[Thundarak]] fallen into a deep slumber.
  - {{year:3200}} A land of [[Pitax]]ian expeditions and research.
  - {{year:3500}} The [[Candle Mages]] founded [[Candelmore]] and were cursed into forgetfulness.
  - {{year:3600}} The city state of [[Oulry]] starts a lucrative trade empire and is destroyed.
  - {{year:3861}} The [[Stag Lord]] starts creating a bandit empire until he is defeated by [[Silvana Ashford]], [[Bree Caley]] and [[Claire Nightshield]] and the [[Golden Lands]] are founded.
