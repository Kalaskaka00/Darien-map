# Boggy Ridge
*The Boggy ridge is a wide mountain range known for its constant rain.*
## Geography
The Boggy Ridge is a mountain range stretching from the [[Nalmarches]]. It divides [[Manchu Tribes]], [[Pitax]] and [[Numeria]] with [[Great Gate]] being the only easy way to pass through.

The Boggy Ridge is not a very tall mountain chain but the rains can make it treacherous with sudden floods and storms.
