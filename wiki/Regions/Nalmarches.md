# Nalmarches
*The Nalmarches is a swampy region dominated by Lizardfolk tribes.*
## Geography
The Nalmarches lay between [[Pitax]], [[Brevoy]] and the [[Golden Lands]]. The swamps are bordered to the east by the [[Stolen Lands]] and to the north by [[Boggy Ridge]].

The terrain is a cold swamp that very difficult to traverse with many small ponds and currents. In the north east lies a more dense woods.
## History
The area has due to its terrain never been settled by a major power but has been culturally significant to the tribes in the area.
