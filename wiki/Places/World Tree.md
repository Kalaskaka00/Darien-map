# World Tree
*The grand tree said to have roots covering most of the continent.*

The World Tree is one of the most essential places to [[Rendeen]] and was for a long time protected personally by [[Evergreen]] in [[Rendeen]]s name. For those with a close connection to Rendeen and plant life it is said that mediating by the tree grants vision over most of the continent.

The World Tree is furthermore said to have a special connection to other realms, especially the [[Dream Realm]].
