# Milor Temple
*Milor temple may well be the grandest temple on the continent with places of prayers for all major deities and many minor deities..*

Milor temple is a grand complex of a temple with many chambers. The temple lays in [[Mivon]]s capital [[Milor]] and holds three different layers of significance to the nation.

Firstly the temple is the main administrative center of [[Mivon]] and [[Milor]] and serves as the main government building and home if the divine protector (leader) of [[Mivon]].

Secondly the temple serves a vital role culturally as at all children are expected to make a pilgrimage to the temple after turning thirteen. In the temple they undergo a ritual that tells them the purpose they have in life. This serves a critical role and determines status and place in society.

Thirdly the temple holds great religious significance being some of the greatest places of worships to most gods. The temple also holds a great many texts and lore on different deities.
