# Stone Road
*Stone Road is one of the old grand fortresses of the [[Dwarven Keeps]] now abondened.*

Stone Road was originally founded to get stonework for the creation of [[Stone Road]] and became a grand fortress during that time. The fortress was abandoned soon after the [[War of the beards]] leaving its ruins to haunt the [[Oulry Hills]].

[[Stone Road]]s ruin has at times been possessed by creatures using it as a base, most notably it was used by [[Chorrad Troll King]] in {{year:3862}}.
