# Bismuth Shrine
*The shrine secret but grand shrines of creation.*

The Bismuth Shrine lays in [[Fain Grasslands]] and is a grand tall structure slowly constructed over many generation. It is dedicated to deities of creating and making like [[Dunden]] and [[Ebus]].

The shrine has for long been maintained by the shamans of the centaur tribes each shaman being responsible for improving and maintaining the shrine. The shrine is not well known and few are allowed to visit it.
