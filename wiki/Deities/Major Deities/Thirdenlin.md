# Thirdenlin
## Description
Thirdenlin is often described as a scholar, wielding a staff, wearing a long cloak and carrying some spell components and a quill. Rendeen is normally described as a male elf.

Thirdenling is often thought of as amoral, knowladgable and infinentley curious. He has a duality in both being knowledable and witty while simeltaniously lacking wisdom and compassion.

Thirdenling is against the concept of "forbidden" knowledge and the meaningful destruction of knowledge. He is thought of as against any limitation of thought and experimentation. He is usually thought of as against war and killing as it is a distraction from higher learning.
## Worship 
Thirdenlin is often worship at altars made in sentagonial shapes with arcane sigills. These altars are often surrounded by bookcases.

A normal way to dedicate one self to Thirdenlin is to write some sort of work or recording some sort of knowledge and adding it to the bookcases around his altars.

Thirdenlin does not have an exact burial ritual, the only real important thing is to record the deads name, years of death and living as well as a few lines about who they were at a special book inside a great altar.
