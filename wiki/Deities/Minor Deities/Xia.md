# Xia
## Description
Xia is often described as a trickster or even malicious storyteller, seeing futures and trying to make certain stories unfold. Xia is often described as handsome young man or woman changing forms and adding a certain word at a certain point to change the fates.

Xia is often described as cunning, curious and heartless. They are often thought of as a character in the background subtly influencing events.

## Worship
Xia has no form of worship.

Xia is often thought of as impossible to find, they find you if they wish to do so.

Xia´s onlu burial rite is that each persons life should be writen down as a short story or told as a short story and that story shall be burried with them.
