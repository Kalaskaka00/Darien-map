Before [[The Wars of Ichor]] another set of Gods reigned than those we are more familiar with. These Gods were less centered around people than the new Gods and were usually more bound to concepts.

This also means they were far harder to understand for the humanoid mind as their goals often seemed incomprehensible. During [[The Wars of Ichor]] most of the Old Gods died and those whom remain are but shadows of them former selves.

After the wards most records of the Old Gods were destroyed leaving them in obscurity, any lore about them is fables and not well known. Some of the Old Gods that have been encountered:
