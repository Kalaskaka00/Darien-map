# The Wars of Ichor
The Wars of Ichor refer to a long period were the Gods fought one another for dominance of the world. On one side of the wars were the [[Old Gods]] who represented animals, nature and other more primal aspects against the New Gods (the current ones) whom represented humanoid life and had gained in power with its rise.

The wars where incredibly destructive killing many people and changing landscapes. Although few details are known.

The wars eventually ended with the [[Old Gods]] defeat and the rise of the New Gods. Since then the [[Old Gods]] have been dead or powerless while the New Gods have become the new power in the cosmos.
