Oulry was a city state nation in the [[Stolen Lands]] that existed between {{year:3593}} and {{year:3666}} during which time it was a profitable trade center. 

The city state eventually fell in to ruins leaving behind the ruins that would become [[Summerborn]]. The city state was felled by [[Nentrah]]s hands, during which time [[Bree Caley]] was rescued by [[Egizaed]].

The city state gots its name from the [[Oulry Hills]] which was the origin of the city state.
