# Mivon independence conflict
Until {{year:3820}} the modern nation of [[Mivon]] was a part of [[Kronharth]] it was however during the [[Period of three Emperors]] and its chaos that the clergy rebelled. The clergy felt it had the divine right and that [[Kronharth]] did not respect the rule of deities, nature and man.

[[Mivon]] declared independence centered around the important [[Milor temple]] and with [[Divine Artifacts|Dundens Divine Blade]]. [[Mivon]] immediately named a hero of [[Mivon]] to lead the charge and wield the blade to secure its independence.

Most nobles in the new nation were quickly slain by the mobs of people riled up by the clergy. Except for the nobility there were only minor skirmishes as [[Kronharth]] could not muster significant resistance. [[Kronharth]] never accepted [[Mivon]]s independence but agreed to cease hostilities in {{year:3822}}.
